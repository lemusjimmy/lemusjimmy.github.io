# About me

My path started in 2004 pulling structured cabling, and today I manage cloud infrastructure at Telefónica Tech. Along the way I worked in support, networking, Windows Server, software testing and digital transformation projects in the financial sector.

That mix gave me something I value: I know the whole stack, from the cable to the cloud. That is why I believe servers should not be pets we look after by hand, but services that can be rebuilt, recover and automate themselves. I enjoy challenging projects, learning continuously and leaving things tidier than I found them.
